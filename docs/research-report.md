# Porting the trader skills to NSE

Adapting the eleven trader-* skills to NSE/BSE is mostly a data-plumbing job, and it is tractable: nearly every US source has a free Indian counterpart that returned data when tested on 29 Oct 2026. Three things are not plumbing. First, the clock: sessions run **09:15-15:30 IST** with no DST, a pre-open call auction from 09:00, and a new Closing Auction Session (CAS) regime that changes the effective close on **7 Dec 2026**. Second, the rules: there is no pattern-day-trader rule, but product type (MIS vs CNC), broker auto square-off (Zerodha: **15:25 normal, 15:12 for CAS stocks, Rs 50 + GST per order**) and an all-in cost of roughly **0.08% per intraday round trip** replace it. Third, trader-pelosi has no legal equivalent, since no source shows India requires MPs to report trades, so it becomes a "who is buying" report built from SEBI insider (PIT) filings, SAST, bulk/block deals and FII/DII flows. The dependable core is NSE's own static files and JSON endpoints plus Yahoo `.NS`; the shakiest parts are anything resting on secondary sources (the CAS dates, tax thresholds, ASM/GSM stage rules) and anything resting on an unofficial or ToS-restricted feed (TradingView scanner, Upstox unauthenticated candles, NSE `/api/*`). Confidence tags used below: **[P]** primary page or endpoint tested/read, **[S]** secondary source only, **[U]** unverified or model background knowledge.

## The clock changes twice before year-end

NSE cash trading is 09:00-09:08 pre-open order entry, 09:08-09:12 matching, 09:12-09:15 buffer, then continuous 09:15-15:30 **[S: search summaries of NSE/BSE pages that could not be opened]** ([NSE pre-open page](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/equity-market-pre-open)). The open is therefore 09:15, not 09:30, so a 15-minute opening range is 09:15-09:30 (5-minute bars at 09:15, 09:20, 09:25), and the "after 9:50 ET" ORB cutoff maps to about **09:35 IST** (the earliest scan) with a classic late cut near 10:00. Timestamps from Yahoo, Kite and Upstox are all start-of-candle; Yahoo uses epoch seconds, Kite and Upstox ISO `+05:30` (tested). IST is UTC+5:30 all year **[U: tz knowledge, no source fetched]**, so `eastern_offset` and the hand-rolled DST logic become a fixed offset or `zoneinfo`.

The close is the awkward part. SEBI's CAS circular of 16 Jan 2026 took effect on **3 Aug 2026 [S]**: for stocks with F&O contracts, continuous trading ends at **15:15**, with the auction after it, while other stocks run to 15:30 ([Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/market-updates/sebi-closing-auction-session-from-august-3-2026-new-timings-and-rules-for-f-o-stocks), [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/nse-extended-fo-timings-and-new-closing-auction-session-rules-explained)). A further SEBI circular dated 15 Oct 2026 reportedly moves CAS to 15:30-15:40 and ends continuous trading at 15:30 for everyone from **7 Dec 2026 [S]** ([Kotak Neo](https://www.kotakneo.com/news/regulations/sebi-revises-closing-auction-rules-derivatives-settlement-methodology/), [Multibagg](https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/sebi-closing-auction-330-340-cmvkv5ufp00062ls0ytxwxery)). Separately, a pre-open change effective **7 Sep 2026** (market and limit orders 09:00-09:05, limit-only 09:05-09:10, matching 09:10-09:12) rests on a single news report with no circular number **[S, weakest]** ([Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/business/nse-pre-open-rules-change-today-can-you-still-place-market-orders-after-905-am)). Sources also disagree on CAS length (some say 15:15-15:30, one says a 15:15-15:20 no-order window) and one outlet says CAS began 4 Aug. None of the NSE, BSE or SEBI circulars could be opened, so every close-time rule should be a dated, configurable table, not a constant. Practical effect: until 4 Dec the last usable continuous bar for F&O-eligible stocks starts 15:10; from 7 Dec it starts 15:25 for all.

Holidays are a data file, not code. 2026 has **16 weekday equity holidays** (15 from NSE's 12 Dec 2025 circular plus an ad hoc 15 Jan municipal-election closure); the list was transcribed from a broker page **[S]** ([Bajaj Broking](https://www.bajajbroking.in/nse-holidays)). Remaining after 29 Oct: **10 Nov (Diwali-Balipratipada), 24 Nov, 25 Dec**. The **Muhurat session is Sunday 8 Nov 2026**, pre-open from 14:05, normal trading 14:20-15:30, all trades settle **[S]** ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/markets/stocks/story/muhurat-trading-2026-nse-bse-announce-diwali-timings-check-full-schedule-562486-2026-10-19)); the skills must treat it as a manual-override open day, and its post-close times are ambiguous in the source. NSE has no regular early-close days (the US 13:00 early closes disappear). The 2027 official list does not exist yet (NSE publishes mid-December); only an incomplete third-party list does ([FinanceCalendar](https://www.financecalendar.com/?p=1454)), so refresh in December. Saturday sessions seen in 2026 were mock/DR drills, not live trading.

## Universe, surveillance filters and data feeds

The US universe problem (Nasdaq Trader files, name-keyword regex to drop ETFs, SPACs and warrants) mostly vanishes. `EQUITY_L.csv` is a clean master of mainboard equity (2,368 EQ, 219 BE, 33 BZ rows on 29 Oct) with no ETFs or SME names **[P, tested HTTP 200 with a browser User-Agent; without one the connection resets]** ([EQUITY_L.csv](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/equities/EQUITY_L.csv)). Base universe = `SERIES == "EQ"`. Liquidity and delivery come from the daily bhavcopy (`sec_bhavdata_full_DDMMYYYY.csv` or the UDiFF zip), which also contains ETFs, REITs and bonds under EQ, so join EQUITY_L first and bhavcopy second ([bhavcopy](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/products/content/sec_bhavdata_full_28102026.csv)). Liquid tiers come from Nifty index CSVs under `https://www.niftyindices.com/IndexConstituent/` (`ind_nifty50list.csv`, `ind_nifty100list.csv`, `ind_nifty200list.csv`, `ind_nifty500list.csv`; Nifty 500 has 500 rows, 496 EQ), all tested. NSE symbols contain `&` and `-` (`M&M`, `BAJAJ-AUTO`) and digit-leading names (`20MICRONS`), so the US regex of 1-5 letters would drop real stocks. Yahoo form is `symbol.NS` with `&` URL-encoded; the TradingView and BSE-on-Yahoo forms are untested **[U]**. BSE has a working UDiFF bhavcopy but no working master-list URL; treat NSE as canonical and BSE as out of scope for v1.

For a day-trading universe, the notes support this conservative filter: series EQ, **not in NSE ASM** (`/api/reportASM`, 190 distinct symbols on 29 Oct), **not in GSM** (`/api/reportGSM`, 77 rows; parse the stage text because combined codes like "GSM - 0" exist), price band 10% or wider or "No Band" (from `sec_list.csv`), and present in Zerodha's public MIS file with multiplier of at least 2 ([api.kite.trade/margins/equity](https://api.kite.trade/margins/equity): 1,672 rows, 1,662 of them 5x; 1,479 of 2,368 EQUITY_L symbols appear). All refresh daily. The notes conflict on how far ASM stages restrict trading: one aggregator says LT-ASM Stage IV moves a stock to trade-for-trade at 100% margin ([circular mirror](https://rhnvrm.github.io/stock-market-circulars/circulars/nse/2025/nse-2025-08-14-4dd248e31dd61457-applicability-of-additional-surveillance-measure-asm)), a broker explainer gives different stage meanings ([Bajaj Broking](https://www.bajajbroking.in/blog/asm-list-nse)), and GSM Stage II buyer-deposit figures differ between a circular and broker FAQs **[S, conflicting]**. The NSE ASM FAQ PDF timed out. The Zerodha file also still showed multiplier above 1 for 60 of 139 current ASM stocks, so use both filters rather than trusting one. BE/BZ series are trade-for-trade, which means no intraday, so exclude them from day scans.

Data sources, ranked by dependability for scan.py and entry.py. Yahoo `.NS` v8 chart worked in one researcher's curl run (120 sequential calls, all 200; `range=60d&interval=5m` returns about 4,500 bars; 1-minute is capped at 8 days) **[P]**. The same note set records a 429 from Yahoo `^NSEI`/`^INDIAVIX` in the main session, and the universe note's own curl got empty output for every symbol. These conflict; the likeliest reading is per-host throttling or an unlucky moment, so keep scan.py's existing query1/query2 fallback and backoff and add a source switch ([yfinance issue 2128](https://github.com/ranaroussi/yfinance/issues/2128)). Second, Upstox's historical-candle endpoint answered **without any token** (`api.upstox.com/v2/historical-candle/NSE_EQ%7C<ISIN>/30minute/...`, 200, `+05:30` stamps) and documents minute data from Jan 2022 and daily from Jan 2000 ([Upstox docs](http://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/v3/get-historical-candle-data/)); whether unauthenticated use is intended or stable is unknown, its 5-minute path was not tested, and its rate-limit page 404'd **[P for 30-minute, U otherwise]**. Third, Zerodha: the free Kite MCP connector lists "historical data for backtesting" ([Zerodha support](https://support.zerodha.com/category/trading-and-markets/general-kite/others-kite/articles/connect-zerodha-ai-assistant)), whereas the Kite Connect API's free Personal tier excludes historical and real-time data and the paid tier is **Rs 500/app/month** ([charges](https://support.zerodha.com/category/trading-and-markets/general-kite/kite-api/articles/what-are-the-charges-for-kite-apis)). Those two statements conflict or at least leave open whether `get_historical_data` works on the free MCP; it needs a logged-in test. If it works, `get_ohlc` polled at about 09:30 gives a one-call opening-range proxy for up to 1,000 symbols (it cannot be backfilled). Kite limits: historical 3 req/s, quote 1 req/s, quote batch 500, per-request day caps (5-minute: 100 days; forum-sourced) ([Kite docs](https://kite.trade/docs/connect/v3/exceptions/)). Fourth, the TradingView scanner (`scanner.tradingview.com/india/scan`) returned all 3,573 NSE rows in one POST with SMA20, ATR, `high|15`/`low|15` and analyst columns, but gives only the current bar, and TradingView's terms license data for "display-only use" and prohibit automated trading and algorithmic decision-making ([TradingView terms](https://www.tradingview.com/policies/)). Use it, if at all, as a human-reviewed fallback, never as a core dependency. NSE `www.nseindia.com/api/*` endpoints worked with a browser User-Agent in the researchers' tests (ASM, GSM, announcements, PIT, SAST, FII/DII) but `quote-equity` returned 403, one note saw the home page 403, and NSE's terms on scripted access could not be retrieved **[P, but unofficial and ToS-unknown]**; static CSVs on `nsearchives.nseindia.com` are the safer path. Angel One, Dhan, Fyers and Groww history limits are unverified.

## Rules, costs and the missing PDT

No source describes a PDT equivalent **[absence of evidence only]**. What replaces it: Kite products CNC (delivery), MIS (intraday margin), NRML (F&O); order types MARKET, LIMIT, SL, SL-M; validity DAY/IOC/TTL ([Kite orders](https://kite.trade/docs/connect/v3/orders/)). Bracket orders were disabled on Kite in March 2020 ([Zerodha support](https://support.zerodha.com/category/trading-and-markets/trading-faqs/general/articles/why-bo-stopped)); whether cover orders are live today is unverified, so the stop must be a separate SL/SL-M order or a GTT. Intraday shorting is possible only as MIS and must be flat by square-off; delivery shorting is not allowed **[U: not fetched]**. Zerodha auto square-off: **non-CAS 15:25, CAS stocks 15:12, F&O 15:26, Rs 50 + 18% GST per squared-off order** ([Zerodha support](https://support.zerodha.com/category/trading-and-markets/trading-faqs/market-sessions/articles/intraday-auto-square-off-timings)). One search summary showed 15:20 on another FAQ; a Dec 2025 post reportedly changed 15:20 to 15:25; the 7 Dec CAS change will probably move these again, so make them configurable and default to "flat by 15:10". The tick is Rs 0.01 under Rs 250, Rs 0.05 for Rs 250-1,000, coarser above ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/capital-market-news/nse-lowers-tick-size-for-stocks-below-rs-250-124052700731_1.html), [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/market-updates/nse-revises-tick-size-for-stocks-and-indices-from-april-15-what-traders-need-to-know)), so cent rounding must become per-stock tick rounding; price bands of 2/5/10/20% mean a stop beyond the day's circuit limit cannot trigger. Cash equity trades in whole shares (no lot sizes), settlement is T+1 **[U]**.

Costs on a Rs 1,00,000 intraday round trip at Zerodha, using brokerage min(0.03%, Rs 20) per order, STT 0.025% sell side, stamp 0.003% buy side, NSE transaction 0.00322%, SEBI Rs 10/crore, GST 18% on brokerage, SEBI and transaction fees: about **Rs 83, or 0.083%** (the worked arithmetic is the notes' own) ([Zerodha charges](https://www.zerodha.com/charges), via search excerpt because the page returned 429; cached snapshots show other exchange rates, effective dates unknown **[S/U]**). Delivery STT 0.1%, stamp 0.015% and DP charge of about Rs 15.34 are **[U]**. Calculator: [Zerodha brokerage calculator](https://zerodha.com/brokerage-calculator).

SEBI's FY25 study found **over 91% of individual F&O traders lost money, average loss about Rs 1.1 lakh** ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/net-losses-of-traders-in-fo-widens-in-fy25-sebi-study-125070701221_1.html)). It is an F&O study; no cash-intraday figure was verified (a roughly 70% FY19 number is **[U]**), so warnings must not stretch it to equities. Budget 2026 raised STT on futures and options, which keeps F&O out of scope ([Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/business/markets/union-budget-2026-stt-on-futures-hiked-by-150-options-by-50-markets-tank-3882160)).

Tax, informational only and almost entirely **[S]**: intraday equity is speculative business income (ITR-3, losses carried forward four years against speculative profit only) per non-official tax blogs ([Sahi](https://www.sahi.com/blogs/income-tax-on-intraday-trading-india)); delivery STCG 20%, LTCG 12.5%, unchanged in Budget 2026 ([Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/news/taxation/union-budget-2026-ltcg-tax-unchanged-at-12-5-stt-on-commodity-futures-hiked)). The Rs 1.25 lakh LTCG exemption, 12-month threshold, Rs 10 crore audit threshold and the Income-tax Act 2025 section renumbering are **[U]**; due dates in blogs conflict (31 Jul vs 31 Aug). The skills should print gross vs after-cost P&L and say nothing more specific about tax. On advice law, SEBI bars unregistered persons from specific recommendations or performance claims, but education is permitted ([IndiaCorpLaw](https://indiacorplaw.in/2025/04/08/margin-call-on-misinformation-sebis-crackdown-on-finfluencers/)). A personal sizing tool fits, with a "not SEBI-registered advice" line. Whether SEBI's 2025 retail-algo framework affects API order placement through Kite was **not researched** and matters for trader-broker.

## News, insider flow and analyst data replace EDGAR and the House Clerk

Corporate announcements come from `https://www.nseindia.com/api/corporate-announcements?index=equities` (per-symbol and date-range filters; fields `an_dt, symbol, desc, attchmntText, attchmntFile, seq_id`; use `seq_id` as the seen key) **[P, tested]**. The results calendar is `/api/event-calendar` (the "earnings date" equivalent), board meetings `/api/corporate-board-meetings`, dividends/splits/bonuses `/api/corporates-corporateActions?...from_date=&to_date=`, shareholding `/api/corporate-share-holdings-master`, and promoter pledge `/api/corporate-pledgedata`. The BSE JSON API returned 403 even with cookies, Referer and Origin, so BSE announcements are unsolved from this host. SEBI publishes `https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebirss.xml` (200, hourly ttl) for regulatory news. Free RSS tested 200: [ET Markets](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/rssfeeds/1977021501.cms), [Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/rss/latestnews.xml) (ISO-8859-1), [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/rss/markets-106.rss), [Mint](https://www.livemint.com/rss/markets), [NDTV Profit](https://feeds.feedburner.com/ndtvprofit-latest), [BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/markets/feeder/default.rss), plus per-ticker [Google News RSS](https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=RELIANCE+NSE+when:1d&hl=en-IN&gl=IN&ceid=IN:en); no free Reuters or PTI feed was found, and publisher terms for reuse were not checked, so keep headline, source, time and link only.

Pre-open context from NSE: `/api/allIndices` (Nifty 50 and India VIX in one call), `/api/marketStatus` (market-open check), `/api/fiidiiTradeReact` (FII/DII provisional, same evening: on 29 Oct FII net -Rs 943.6 crore, DII +Rs 1,924.41 crore), participant OI `fao_participant_oi_DDMMYYYY.csv`, and the F&O ban list `https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/fo/fo_secban.csv` (published a day ahead; ban at 95% of market-wide position limit, exit under 80% **[S]**). GIFT Nifty and US overnight came from the TradingView global scan (`NSEIX:NIFTY1!`), again subject to its terms. NSDL's FPI page differs from NSE's provisional FII figure on the same day (+1,113 vs -943.6 crore), so the two series must never be mixed ([NSDL](https://www.fpi.nsdl.co.in/web/Reports/Latest.aspx)).

For trader-pelosi, the evidence for "no Indian equivalent" is old and secondary (MPs file asset declarations, not trades) ([Moneylife](https://www.moneylife.in/article/65-ls-members-29-rs-members-yet-to-declare-assets-reveals-rti/55194.html)), and no current post-2018 source on Parliament's rules was found. The replacement: SEBI PIT Reg 7(2) disclosures by promoters, directors and designated persons above Rs 10 lakh per quarter, filed within 2 trading days **[S]** ([Cyril Amarchand Mangaldas](https://corporate.cyrilamarchandblogs.com/2017/05/sebis-informal-guidance-continual-disclosures-prevention-insider-trading-regulations/)), served at `/api/corporates-pit` (fields `acqName, personCategory, tdpTransactionType, secAcq, secVal, acqMode`; the sample showed a 5-day trade-to-posting lag); SAST Reg 29 at `/api/corporate-sast-reg29`; bulk and block deals at `/api/snapshot-capital-market-largedeal` and `nsearchives.../bulk.csv`, `block.csv`. Bulk deal is 0.5% of listed shares (blogs only); block deal minimum Rs 25 crore per NSE's Dec 2025 FAQ, with older blogs still saying Rs 10 crore ([NSE FAQ](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com//web/mediaattachment/2025-12/FAQs_on_Block_Deal_Session_20251205105730.pdf)). A serious anomaly: the PIT endpoint returned **empty data for every Jul-Oct 2026 date range** while Feb 2026 worked, and symbol-only calls returned stale rows. Cause unknown, so the skill must print "no filings returned", never "no insider buying", and must be re-tested on a normal trading day.

For trader-analyst, nothing replaces SEC XBRL one-for-one. NSE announcements carry `hasXbrl` for results filings, but the XBRL structure and URL scheme were not fetched, and Indian companies report a standalone Q4, so the SEC "derived Q4" logic goes. Consensus targets and buy/hold/sell counts came from the TradingView scanner (e.g. RELIANCE mean target 1,640.79, 18 buy of 19; TCS 21 buy, 14 hold, 4 sell), which works unauthenticated but sits under the same non-display restriction; `analyst_count` was null and the meanings of `recommendation_over/under` are guessed. Trendlyne's terms bar machine-readable databases ([Trendlyne terms](https://trendlyne.com/terms/)); Screener.in's company pages load (200) but its terms bar copying and it was not shown to carry broker targets ([Screener terms](https://www.screener.in/guides/terms/)). Morningstar requires OAuth, so was not used.

For trader-alerts, Kite's alerts API (simple and order-placing `ato` types, 500 active alerts) and GTT API (`single` and `two-leg` OCO) are documented ([alerts](https://kite.trade/docs/connect/v3/alerts/), [GTT](https://kite.trade/docs/connect/v3/gtt/)); the free tier reportedly covers them but only third-party pages say so **[S]**. The Zerodha MCP in this environment already exposes `get_gtts`, `place_gtt_order`, `get_ltp`, `get_quotes` and `get_holdings` (tool names only, not exercised). Telegram delivery limits and WhatsApp were not researched.

## Recommended build order

First, a shared `market_india.json` plus clock helper (sessions per date, holidays, Muhurat override, square-off times); everything depends on it. Second, trader-scanner (universe and filters, then the daily scan, then ORB), since it feeds the rest and exercises the data-source switch. Third, trader-entry and trader-exit together (same bars, same tick and circuit logic). Fourth, trader-budget and trader-risk (cost stack, square-off check, Kite order shape). Fifth, trader-broker on the Zerodha MCP, built last of the order path and only after the SEBI algo question is read, with a first live session limited to tiny size. In parallel with the order path: trader-news (NSE announcements are well tested), then trader-alerts (ban/ASM diffs), then trader-timemachine (needs the cost stack and clock). Last: trader-pelosi replacement (blocked on the PIT anomaly) and trader-analyst (blocked on XBRL structure and the TradingView terms decision).

## Conclusion

The research changes the framing from "convert US endpoints to Indian ones" to "decide how much to lean on unofficial feeds". NSE's static files and a handful of JSON endpoints give a better surveillance and corporate-action picture than the US skills had, but nearly everything for quotes, history and analyst data sits on sources with no stable contract (Yahoo, TradingView, Upstox, NSE `/api/*`), so a swappable data layer is the main design requirement. The second novelty is that regulation is moving under the skills: two CAS regimes and a pre-open change land between September and December 2026, so dated, data-driven session rules are cheaper than any constant. Settling the open items in order (the two circulars, the Kite MCP history test, the PIT anomaly) removes most of the remaining risk.
